MintAct: A Unified Visual Agent for Digital Environments.

Mingfei Gao*, Rui Tian*, Haiming Gang*, Bohan Zhai*, Le Zhang, Yuanzheng Gong, Di Feng, Ege Özsoy, Kaixin Ma, Vishwesh Kirthivasan, Oğuzhan Fatih Kar, Roman Bachmann, Anders Boesen Lindbo Larsen, Afshin Dehghan.

Preprint 2026

[Paper]

Research summary

We present MintAct, a family of vision-language models that unifies UI grounding, multi-step navigation, and visual tool use across mobile, desktop, and web environments. We develop scalable environments and asynchronous reinforcement learning to train generalist agents that match domain specialists and achieve 48.9 on OSWorld-Verified.

UniGen-1.5: Enhancing Image Generation and Editing through Reward Unification in Reinforcement Learning.

Rui Tian, Mingfei Gao, Haiming Gang, Jiasen Lu, Zhe Gan, Yinfei Yang, Zuxuan Wu, Afshin Dehghan.

CVPR 2026

[Paper]

Research summary

We present UniGen-1.5 for unified image understanding, generation, and editing. A shared-reward reinforcement learning strategy jointly improves generation and editing, while a lightweight Edit Instruction Alignment stage strengthens instruction comprehension.

UniGen: Enhanced Training & Test-Time Strategies for Unified Multimodal Understanding and Generation.

Rui Tian*, Mingfei Gao*, Mingze Xu*, Jiaming Hu, Jiasen Lu, Zuxuan Wu, Yinfei Yang, Afshin Dehghan.

NeurIPS 2025

[Paper] [Code]

Research summary

We propose UniGen, a unified multimodal large language model for both understanding and generation, and design a new Chain-of-Thought Verification (CoT-V) strategy by enabling it to act as both an image generator and a step-by-step image quality verifier.

REDUCIO! Generating 1024 × 1024 Video within 16 Seconds using Extremely Compressed Motion Latents.
